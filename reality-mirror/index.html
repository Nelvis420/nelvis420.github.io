OPENING CAMERA…

Camera needs a tap of permission

Reality View Mirror uses your camera to show you live. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere — the picture never leaves this screen. Allow camera access, then tap Retry.

  1. Click the camera icon — or the lock / tune icon — at the left end of your browser's address bar, at the top of the window.
  2. Find Camera in the site permissions that open, and set it to Allow for this site.
  3. Come back here and tap Retry below.
